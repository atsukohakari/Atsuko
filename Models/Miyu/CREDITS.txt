changes made for Atsuko Hub: the left leg was straightened, the model was cut into
body parts by its bones (skirt front, skirt back and water bottle are their own parts
so they can swing), the outline meshes and ground were left out, the hidden trash can
was split into can / lid / window, textures were resized to 1024, and everything was
converted to Roblox mesh files (version 2.00) plus model.json.
