v1.1.1
- new ai tab: ask for a script or how to fix one and it writes it for you, with copy and open in editor buttons on the code
- the ai can read the script in your editor: tap the code button next to the box and it goes with your message
- the ai is called Atsuko Hakari and has its own picture. its name is the big title on the ai tab, change the name and picture whenever you want
- personal preferences: tell the ai about you and how you want your answers, in settings > ai or in the ai's own settings. it reads them before every answer
- recent chats: your last 30 chats are saved, search them, open one to keep going or delete it (the clock button at the top of the ai tab)
- ai settings with 4 tabs: profile, preferences, ai key and chats. answer length, creativity, reply language, chat memory, remember chats and a heads-up when an answer is ready
- clean chat look: your messages on the right with your picture, answers with the ai's picture, copy and retry under each answer, and ideas to start with
- the ai works with a free google gemini key, or any openai-style service (openrouter, groq and more). your keys stay on your device
- the ai lives in its own file and only downloads when you open the tab, so the hub doesn't get bigger. an old copy of that file never opens on a newer hub, the tab tells you to update it
- pick your own profile picture in settings > general (a roblox image id or a picture link), it shows on home and in the ai chat
- search youtube for songs in music > add, then add them with one tap (needs a free youtube key, songs go through your converter)
- the command bar opens as soon as you press ; (or your own key), and the key doesn't end up in the box
- keys can be combos now: shift + ; makes : and the other shift symbols work too (! ? @ and more), plus ctrl + any key
- hub keys work even when the game uses the same key, just not while you're typing or in the roblox menu
- your converter and youtube keys are never in copy my settings
- the owner's chat tag says Atsuko Hakari now
- removed the colored fog and aurora sky commands
